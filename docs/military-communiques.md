# Dispatches from the Glorious Surface

<div class="mp-kicker">MINISTRY OF DEFENSE · PUBLIC MORALE COPY</div>

> **EVERY ENGAGEMENT IS A VICTORY. THE REMAINDER IS LOGISTICS.**

These documents are fictional notices and mission-flavor material. The arcade game should stay fast and readable; deep context belongs in optional archives.

## Dispatch 001: The first deployment

The Motherplant extends Her steel hand to the wasteland. Recovery Group Dawn has been delivered safely to the surface. The crews report excellent morale, clear sights and a gratifying abundance of targets. May the choir carry them home.

**PUBLIC RESULT:** OPERATION BEGUN SUCCESSFULLY.

## Dispatch 014: Friendly silhouettes

Multiple targets observed bearing recognized allied markings. A revised identification directive has confirmed their hostile nature. All units are congratulated for immediately remembering that these vehicles have always been enemies.

**REDACTED UNDER MOD-IFS-014**

## Dispatch 026: Seismic event

A large subterranean object entered the area of operations without appropriate surface access permits. The object deployed weapon systems and resisted a mandatory inspection. Recovery crews are ordered to disable it and secure the contained CORE.

**PUBLIC RESULT:** ANOTHER INDUSTRIAL OBSTRUCTION REMOVED.

## Dispatch 031: Losses and replacements

The Ministry is pleased to announce that all units assigned to the 31st Recovery Detachment have completed their service. Requests for information regarding personnel transfers should be submitted to the Reassignment Office, currently closed for reprocessing.

**PUBLIC RESULT:** THE DETACHMENT HAS NO REMAINING OBLIGATIONS.

## Dispatch 042: Unexpected cooperation

Our longstanding comrades in Muhosransk have reaffirmed their historic allegiance to the Motherplant. Previous military records incorrectly depicting hostilities were inserted by dissident archivists.

**PUBLIC RESULT:** ONE HUNDRED YEARS OF FRIENDSHIP RECONFIRMED.

## Writers' note

Propaganda should praise courage and competence sincerely. The military machine is simultaneously formidable and epistemically broken. Soldiers may be brave, skilled, decent people who are tragically wrong about whom they are fighting.
