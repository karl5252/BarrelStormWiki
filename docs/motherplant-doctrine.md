# The Doctrine of the Motherplant

<div class="mp-kicker">MINISTRY OF SOCIAL HARMONY · AUTHORIZED CIVIC TEXT</div>

## Mother provides

Before the light, there was the cold. Before the furnace, there was hunger. Before the Motherplant, there was uncertainty.

Mother brought warmth beneath the ice. She laid her roots through stone, carried power into forgotten halls, and made a home where the surface offered none. The People were not abandoned. The People were gathered.

**The Motherplant provides. The People endure.**

## Every hand serves

A tanker does not fight alone. Behind every engine stand a thousand hands: the miner, the welder, the keeper of the furnace, the recorder of quotas, and the choir that gives the march its voice.

Their labor accompanies every shell. Their faith rides in every track.

The tank crew does not seek war. It performs the sacred duty of resource recovery. Where obstacles arise, they are cleared. Where CORES are found, they are returned.

**The Earth provides. The Motherplant collects.**

## Our enemies and our friends

The Motherplant has always known who stands with the People. Changes to official enemy designations do not constitute changes to history; they reflect improvements in public understanding.

Citizens are reminded that previous announcements remain accurate for the periods during which they were approved.

**LOYALTY REQUIRES FLEXIBILITY.**

## Mother knows

When the lights dim, it is conservation. When a district falls silent, it has completed its service. When the calendar stands still, the year has been granted additional opportunity for productivity.

Mother does not forget her children. Records are retained for as long as their continued existence serves the People.

**THE MOTHERPLANT HAS NEVER CONTRADICTED HERSELF.**

## The oath of deployment

> I was sheltered by Mother.  
> I was sustained by her furnaces.  
> I will carry her strength upon the surface.  
> I will return the CORE entrusted to the wasteland.  
> I will not mistake uncertainty for disobedience.  
> I will serve until recalled.  
> **FOR THE MOTHERPLANT.**
