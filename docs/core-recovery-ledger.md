## Public accounting

A CORE is a precious energy component. In state broadcasts it is described not as fuel, but as warmth returned to its rightful keeper. The recovery of a CORE is celebrated with music, commendations and revised production targets.

The ledger records every recovery as a triumph. An entry for the district that lost the CORE is optional.

## The real dilemma

The Mothercore is degrading and hungry for CORES. More input may keep critical systems alive, but the removal of a CORE can condemn a distant settlement. Her failing diagnostics may record the gain without accurately measuring the loss. A victorious crew can unknowingly make the network less stable.

Not every CORE need be stolen from a friendly branch. Some may come from hostile outsiders, derelict machines, or genuinely dangerous rogue systems. Preserve ambiguity about any specific encounter unless the story demands an answer.
