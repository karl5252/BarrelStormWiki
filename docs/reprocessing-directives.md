## Directive 01: The permanence of standards

Classification standards have never changed. Where two authentic documents carry incompatible codes, the more recent approved document is evidence that the earlier document was maliciously misfiled.

- `NGIE7923652398`: valid, pending validation.
- `774777737JJ3`: also valid, independently and eternally.
- `MOTHER-APPROVED-0000`: always in use, even before its introduction.

Requests for a conversion table are to be rejected. Translation implies change. Change implies error. Error is not within the Ministry's mandate.

## Directive 02: Material conservation

The Ministry does not destroy records. Documents may be repurposed into heating fuel, insulation, parade confetti, classroom paper flowers or the covers of new history books.

The preservation of accurate history does not require the preservation of every inaccurate object.

## Directive 03: Former allies

When a previously hostile state is identified as a historic partner, the following tasks shall be completed before the next morning bulletin:

1. Replace hostile insignia on public maps.
2. Distribute corrected schoolroom charts.
3. Adjust victory parade speeches to celebrate the partnership.
4. Recover and reprocess obsolete posters, including those printed that morning.
5. Congratulate citizens for remembering what has always been true.

## Directive 04: Public announcements

A reassuring announcement shall remain reassuring even when its subject no longer exists. The absence of a district is not sufficient reason to interrupt its broadcast.

## Directive 05: Interpretive safety

Personnel who observe mutually incompatible facts must not attempt unauthorized reconciliation. The appropriate response is to request corrected facts. An institution's duty is not to make reality confusing; it is to protect citizens from confusion caused by reality.
