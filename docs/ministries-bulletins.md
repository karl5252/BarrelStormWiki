## Notice 2255-A: Essential reassurance

The Motherplant has assessed the recent disturbances and determined that no emergency exists. Citizens are requested to avoid windows during the non-emergency interval.

## Notice 2255-B: Timely gratitude

The Ministry wishes all citizens a prosperous second year of 2255. The introduction of an additional 2255 is evidence of unprecedented calendar efficiency. There was no first second year; the present year has always been correctly numbered.

## Notice 2255-C: Friendship with Muhosransk

The heroic population of Muhosransk has stood alongside Mother for one hundred uninterrupted years. Reports of a historical conflict are enemy propaganda.

## Notice 2255-D: Domestic heating

Households reporting low temperatures are encouraged to verify their thermostat's official certification. Unauthorized thermometers may display unapproved degrees.

## Notice 2255-E: Recovery figures

A record number of CORES have been successfully returned to the Motherplant. Any corresponding increase in CORE requirements reflects the success of our recovery program, not a deficit.

## Notice 2255-F: Literary hygiene

Documents bearing anomalous classification marks should be surrendered for reprocessing. The standards have never changed. Suspicious records may be turned into useful exercise books for the coming Mother's Day celebrations.

## Notice 2255-G: Voluntary participation

Attendance at the Annual Gratitude Assembly is voluntary. Non-attendance must be registered in person at the assembly entrance.

## Notice 2255-H: False alarms

In response to a series of contradictory civil defense broadcasts, residents are reminded that a correctly functioning alarm system may sound without indicating any danger. Similarly, a silent alarm does not imply safety. In all cases remain reassured.
