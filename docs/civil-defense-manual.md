## Recognizing an officially recognized emergency

A civil-defense emergency exists only after its existence has been authenticated. Audible detonations, enemy vehicles and unexplained structural damage are insufficient evidence on their own.

Citizens should obey the nearest functioning announcement system. If two systems disagree, follow the most recent officially verified message. If their dates are identical, remain in place until clarification becomes available.

## Shelter procedure

1. Bring your household identification and approved ration papers.
2. Leave unnecessary possessions behind. Family photographs are permitted if they contain no obsolete insignia.
3. Enter the designated shelter. Do not occupy a shelter assigned to another district without prior permission.
4. Await the all-clear signal. An interrupted alarm is not an all-clear.
5. Where heating is unavailable, conserve warmth by remaining optimistic.

!!! note "Important"
    Shelters are inspected regularly according to the published schedule. An inspection record is considered evidence of an inspection, including when the inspecting unit is no longer in service.

## Conflicting announcements

In the unlikely event that one district reports attack while another reports peace, both services are functioning as designed. Citizens should not attempt to reconcile separate jurisdictions' lived experiences without appropriate credentials.

## If your shelter has been forgotten

A shelter that has not received an all-clear remains under protection. Do not conclude that the Motherplant has abandoned you. Inquiries about delayed rescue should be directed to the district office during regular opening hours.

*Office hours are subject to power availability.*
