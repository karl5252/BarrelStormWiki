## Case 008: The two flags

A parade storage facility contained two approved banners celebrating mutually exclusive alliances. Both displayed authentic Ministry approval seals. The first banner was designated a commemorative reproduction. The second was designated the original. The originals were then exchanged for improved commemorative originals.

**RESOLUTION:** No contradiction existed at any point.

## Case 019: A mother's letter

A citizen requested the service record of her son. Three offices confirmed that he was alive, reassigned, and posthumously decorated. She asked which certificate she should display in the kitchen. The Ministry advised all three, provided the frames were equal in size.

**RESOLUTION:** Family satisfaction assumed.

## Case 027: Incorrect snowfall

A district weather log reported temperatures below the certified minimum. Officials confiscated the thermometer. Snow continued accumulating inside a transit hall. The snowfall was ruled a decorative powder used during Mother's Day preparations.

**RESOLUTION:** Weather anomaly eliminated from records.

## Case 044: Muhosransk education file

A drawing titled *Mother Keeps Us Warm* was recovered from a closed classroom. The paper showed a reactor, a family and twenty-eight children. The associated attendance register listed twenty-eight children. The corresponding population registry listed zero.

**RESOLUTION:** Artwork approved. Attendance register withheld.

## Case 061: The unreliable cartographer

A surveyor drew an underground tunnel crossing two districts whose official maps were separated by hundreds of kilometers. Trains later arrived on the same tunnel. The surveyor's map was classified as misleading because it used distances unsupported by the Ministry.

**RESOLUTION:** Tunnel permitted. Map destroyed.

## Case 077: Revised classification standard

An archivist claimed that the codes `NGIE7923652398` and `774777737JJ3` belonged to incompatible filing systems. Both documents were verified as originating from the same eternal standard. The archivist was reassigned to the paper mill to study the process of material continuity.

**RESOLUTION:** Standard unchanged. Personnel improved.

### Policy for handling genuine evidence

Citizens finding contradictory documents shall report them immediately. The Ministry accepts books, photographs, mechanical tapes, historical records, signed orders and personal memories. Physical objects will be returned where practicable. Memories will be returned in corrected form.
