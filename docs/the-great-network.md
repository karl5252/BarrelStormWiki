# The Great Network

<div class="mp-kicker">DIRECTORATE OF SUBTERRANEAN HARMONY · INFRASTRUCTURE REGISTER</div>

> **WHERE THE ROOTS REACH, MOTHER PROVIDES.**

## Official explanation

The Motherplant's subterranean transport and energy system joins the People in an unbroken embrace. Rails, power conduits, furnace galleries, drilling assemblies and deployment cradles conduct resources exactly where required.

The network has no recognized boundary. Surveys of its outermost extent are unnecessary while central certification remains valid.

## The industrial mycelium

*Restricted setting notes.*

Imagine a fungal root system reconstructed in steel. Its 'hyphae' are cables, pipes, tunnels, segmented machines and railway arteries. Its nodes may be factories, settlements, reactor chambers, mobile platforms or forgotten machinery. A tank's deployment lift may be a shaft, a train, or the tip of an articulated appendage; operators rarely see beyond their own station.

The surface battlefield is merely the visible layer of a vast organism. Tank units act like immune cells, deployed to intercept incursions and recover resources. As the Mothercore's recognition systems decay, immune responses increasingly target her own infrastructure.

## Peripheral divergence

The network does not fail uniformly. Different appendages may:

- stop acknowledging central orders but continue protecting local inhabitants;
- follow an obsolete yet internally consistent directive;
- cannibalize neighboring machinery to preserve vital functions;
- attack other Motherplant units after a damaged identification update;
- hide resources from the Mothercore, or attempt to heal her.

**Rogue does not mean evil.** 'Loyal' does not guarantee safe. Official classifications tell us more about the current command system than about the machine in question.

## For the arcade game

Keep the immense network mostly **unseen**. Suggest it through deployment mechanisms, trembling terrain, impossible underground machinery and gigantic CORE carriers erupting through the surface. No giant map revealing the entire organism; no cutscene explaining its diagnosis.

## Unresolved

Where does the network end? Are all its roots descended from the original power plant? Can severed appendages become genuinely independent? Does the Mothercore understand how much of her body she has already lost?
