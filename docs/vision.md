# Game Vision & Design Pillars

## One-sentence pitch

**BarrelStorm** is a high-impact, top-down arcade tank game about roaming an endless wasteland to recover precious CORES for a fanatical underground industrial power known as **the Motherplant**.

## The fantasy

The Motherplant does not ask whether the mission is justified. Its underground infrastructure delivers your tank to the surface, where you roam ruined landscapes, engage hostile armor, and hunt enormous subterranean war machines. When one of these colossal machines erupts from below, its destruction can yield a precious CORE.

**FOR THE MOTHERPLANT!**

## Confirmed direction

- **No city-assault or civilian-targeting premise.** The combat takes place in hostile wasteland, not populated city centers.
- **Endless chunk-generated battlefield.** Roaming is central, rather than clearing a sequence of fixed urban arenas.
- **Tank-centered arcade combat.** Ordinary enemies include tanks in different sizes and roles.
- **Subterranean deployment.** A massive underground base or machine delivers the player tank onto the battlefield. Its precise engineering and name are undecided.
- **Giant CORE-carrying encounters.** An enormous subterranean enemy rises through the battlefield. Destroying it releases a valuable CORE.
- **Split aesthetic.** Menus, propaganda and fiction retain the neo-Soviet, quasi-religious Motherplant identity; in-game action leans into the silhouette-driven clarity and explosive energy of *Luftrausers*.

## Design pillars

1. **Move, fight, keep moving.** Roaming and rapid engagement should dominate the player's time.
2. **Recognize threats instantly.** Tank size, silhouette, movement and effects communicate danger without forcing players to read UI panels.
3. **Make the earth feel alive.** The underground is not a passive backdrop: deployment and colossal enemy emergence are key spectacles.
4. **Make CORES matter.** A CORE is a rare, dramatic payoff, not another indistinguishable pickup. Its exact economy is still open.
5. **Let propaganda do the storytelling.** The Motherplant's institutional voice should be unmistakable but need not interrupt combat with exposition.

## Reference DNA (not literal copies)

- **Luftrausers:** fast arcade rhythm, readable combat, bold effects, and large special enemy encounters.
- **Mortal Engines:** colossal industrial machines stalking a devastated environment, reimagined here as subterranean armored megastructures instead of moving cities.
- **Neo-Soviet industrial satire:** brutalist machinery, slogans, bureaucratic euphemism and a state-like machine cult.

## Explicitly retired direction

The player is **not** sent to invade populated cities or attack civilians. That earlier concept should not be treated as current design canon.

## Not yet decided

- Extraction and return-to-base rules
- Whether a mission ends, escalates indefinitely or supports voluntary retreat
- Exact CORE uses and persistence
- Specific enemy roster, bosses, factions and biomes
- Upgrade balance and tank handling values
