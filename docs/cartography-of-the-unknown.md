> **THE NETWORK HAS NO UNMAPPED PARTS. SOME PARTS ARE NOT CURRENTLY ON THE MAP.**

## A map is an order

Most official maps show routes, not geography. A corridor may be labeled **ACTIVE** after collapsing, because trains are still scheduled to use it. A functioning tunnel may be marked **NONEXISTENT** because the Ministry lacks a valid survey request.

The Mothercore cannot be reliably mapped as one structure. It may stretch for miles beneath frozen ground through countless rails, roots and industrial chambers. Surface tank deployment sites are merely points where a limb touches the wasteland.

## Three contradictory surveys

**Survey A / Year 2255:** The eastern mechanical root terminates 19 kilometers beyond the Red Orchard.

**Survey B / Year 2255:** The eastern root has never approached the Red Orchard. It extends toward Muhosransk.

**Survey C / Year 2255:** No eastern root is recorded. A maintenance convoy nevertheless traveled along it that morning.

All three surveys carry valid stamps. The surveyors have never met.

## The station that moves

Certain platforms appear on timetables only when a train needs to stop there. Passengers describe endless black windows, giant cable bundles and mechanical movement beyond the lamps. On the return trip, the platform's name has changed. The ticket remains valid.

Whether the platform moves, the tracks rearrange, or the navigation system sends passengers to the wrong place should remain unknown.

## A cartographer's final margin note

> We have mapped the Motherplant as if she were a building. But a building does not extend a passage to meet a lost train. A building does not close an artery behind the survey crew. I do not think the tunnels are where they were yesterday. I do not know whether that means the maps are wrong or that Mother is moving.
