## A welcome from Mother

Dear child,

If you are reading this, you have been granted warmth, shelter and the remarkable privilege of literacy. Each of these gifts was made possible by the Motherplant. Remember Her in your daily work, and She will remember you in the next service allocation.

You need not be exceptional to be useful. You need only be correctly assigned.

## Your morning responsibilities

1. Check the Ministry's approved date. Do not compare it with yesterday's date without written authorization.
2. Confirm that your household heating indicator shows **SUFFICIENT**. This indication is authoritative regardless of ambient temperature.
3. Repeat the daily bulletin with appropriate enthusiasm. Quiet enthusiasm is acceptable in sleeping quarters.
4. Report unidentified noises in service shafts. Do not investigate. Mother's maintenance apparatus values privacy.
5. Thank the Motherplant for the continued operation of your lungs, whether or not ventilation services are available.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does Mother need CORES?**

A mother does not *need*. She receives what Her children gratefully return.

**Why has the neighboring district gone dark?**

The district has achieved a superior state of energy discipline. Do not disturb its residents during their conservation interval.

**Can a friend become an enemy?**

No. Citizens sometimes discover that a person previously designated friendly has always been an enemy. Such discoveries demonstrate the perfection of the identification process.

**What if I remember an earlier announcement?**

Memory is a natural resource. Like all resources, it benefits from professional reprocessing.

**What if Mother is wrong?**

This question contains a formatting error. Submit it again after completing the approved civics course.

## The three assurances

**She shelters us.** Where Her roots extend, there is a home.

**She remembers us.** Administrative retention periods may vary.

**She will not leave us.** Reassignment of infrastructure does not constitute departure.

## The duty of helpful observation

A concerned citizen reports possible inaccuracies in maps, clocks, insignia and testimony. Reports must be filed against the *observation*, never against the Ministry. If the discrepancy is confirmed, the observing citizen may be invited to attend a voluntary corrective seminar.
