# The Motherplant: World, Fiction & Tone

## Central idea

The **Motherplant** is an underground industrial authority and the ideological heart of BarrelStorm. Its machinery, production quotas and CORES are treated with near-religious seriousness. The fiction suggests a neo-Soviet society where state bureaucracy, industrial dependence and worship-like ritual have blurred together.

The game's surface world is a hostile wasteland filled with rival armor and dangerous machines, **not a civilian population to be conquered**.

## Two layers, one identity

**Framing layer:** propaganda posters, menu text, mission briefings, mechanical iconography and institutional slogans can be pompous, doctrinal, ritualistic and absurd.

**Battlefield layer:** action is direct and almost wordless. The player sees tracks, dust, muzzle flashes, wrecks, tanks and colossal machines surfacing from the ground. The fiction informs the world without smothering the controls.

## Tone rules

- Satirize **institutions and war bureaucracy**, not civilian suffering.
- Keep the Motherplant simultaneously ominous and absurd.
- Let repetition, slogans and official euphemism imply a larger ideology.
- Avoid turning every line into a joke: deadpan delivery makes the absurdity stronger.
- Avoid detailed exposition during high-speed combat.

## Propaganda language: sample copy

These are **example slogans**, not fixed mission dialogue:

> THE MOTHERPLANT NEEDS YOU.
>
> THE EARTH PROVIDES. THE MOTHERPLANT COLLECTS.
>
> RESISTANCE IS A LOGISTICAL INCONVENIENCE.
>
> YOUR SACRIFICE HAS BEEN APPROVED BY THE MINISTRY OF PRODUCTION.
>
> FOR THE MOTHERPLANT! THE QUOTA MUST BE MET.

## Questions for future worldbuilding

- Is the Motherplant one physical installation or a network of underground facilities?
- Who built the giant subterranean CORE carriers, and why do they contain CORES?
- Do enemy tanks answer to rival Motherplants, autonomous systems or unknown powers?
- Does the Motherplant genuinely preserve civilization, or only perpetuate its own machinery?

These mysteries can remain unanswered in the game longer than in the design notes.
