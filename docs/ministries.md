# Ministries of the Motherplant

<div class="mp-kicker">OFFICE OF ADMINISTRATIVE CONTINUITY · ORGANIZATIONAL HANDBOOK</div>

> **THE MOTHERPLANT DOES NOT MAKE MISTAKES. SHE MAKES RAW MATERIAL.**

The Ministries turn the Mothercore's shifting directives into one reassuring public reality. They are not a collection of cartoon villains: many clerks sincerely believe their work is essential to civilization's survival. Their tragedy is that the machinery of certainty leaves no place for evidence.

## Ministry of Public Clarity

Publishes bulletins, validates friendly and hostile designations, and ensures that contradictory announcements are never simultaneously considered authentic.

**Approved wording:** 'Our allies have always been our allies. Yesterday's opposing announcement was a dissident reproduction.'

## Ministry of Production

Maintains quotas, distributes resources and records the glorious return of CORES. An unmet quota is reclassified as an advance contribution to the next reporting interval.

**Approved wording:** 'Scarcity is an unauthorized description of targeted abundance redistribution.'

## Ministry of Chronological Stability

Publishes the official year and reconciles the calendar with the Mothercore's clock. It has **always** used the current classification and dating standards. Older formats require no conversion when they can instead be identified as forgeries.

**Approved wording:** 'The year 2255 has been extended in recognition of extraordinary productivity.'

## Ministry of Defense

Dispatches armored recovery units, certifies enemy silhouettes and resolves battlefield identification discrepancies. Troops reporting attacks by allied formations may be ordered to await confirmation even while under fire.

**Approved wording:** 'A friendly unit remains friendly until appropriately redesignated.'

## Ministry of Maternal Welfare

Operates schools, heating provision, public health and Mother's Day observances. Children are taught that Mother gave them warmth; they make paper banners and tiny plasticine reactors to thank her.

**Approved wording:** 'Mother watches over you, even when local monitoring services are temporarily unavailable.'

## Ministry of Cultural Reprocessing

Maintains archives, approves posters and songbooks, and turns invalid documents into useful material. The Ministry does not censor history. It reprocesses inaccurate objects so that the correct history may be preserved.

**Approved wording:** 'No information has been destroyed. Its continued storage was judged nonproductive.'

## Documentary standards

Classification marks can include arbitrary codes such as `NGIE7923652398` or `774777737JJ3`. They are not inconsistent: the standard has always been uniform. If a code cannot be interpreted, the originating document may be dissident material.

> REPRODUCTION AUTHORIZED: ████████\
> **REDACTED UNDER 774777737JJ3**

## In-world tone

Ministers speak politely, patiently and with terrifying confidence. Their proclamations should be bureaucratically plausible before they become absurd. No ministry should announce that it is evil, rewriting history or lying. It has merely completed another successful correction.
