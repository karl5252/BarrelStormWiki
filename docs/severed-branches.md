## Official classifications

- **Dormant:** An appendage temporarily unavailable for useful service.
- **Divergent:** An appendage returning reports in an unapproved format.
- **Rogue:** An appendage whose loyalty has been corrected by the Ministry of Defense.
- **Recovered:** An appendage whose CORE is now in authorized custody.

Classification does not describe whether the inhabitants are safe, whether machinery functions, or whether the central order is sensible.

## The Listening Root

A long mechanical branch keeps its lights off and opens its gates only when it hears human voices. Locals claim it learned to distinguish living people from command transmissions and refuses orders that would vent inhabited chambers. The Network Authority labels it *hostile to central coordination*.

Nobody knows whether it is conscious. Children in neighboring settlements leave tiny brass bells where its pipes emerge.

## Branch Ash-17

Once a refinery spur, now a nest of autonomous machines that strip approaching vehicles for repair materials. Their identification transponders broadcast an ancient authorization recognized by no current ministry. They attack Motherplant patrols but leave unarmed supply sledges untouched.

Does this mean compassion, an obsolete cargo filter, or a broken sensor? The answer is not specified.

## The Gentle Furnace

A remote heating node that diverted all spare energy from its own repair systems into the neighboring residential sector. Its pipes are failing, but the apartments remain warm. Central Command repeatedly orders it to surrender its last CORE for redistribution. It declines, always in respectful language.

**STATUS:** ROGUE. **REASON:** INSUBORDINATION THROUGH EXCESSIVE CARE.

## The Empty Hand

An enormous articulated carrier rises into the wasteland and extends a claw toward armored units. It does not fire unless fired upon. Its audio beacon repeats a maintenance request in an obsolete dialect of machine code. It may be a broken limb seeking repair, an enemy trap, or an isolated part of Mother trying to reconnect.

No single canonical answer is required. To an arcade player, it can still be an intimidating encounter; a tabletop party may decide not to shoot.

## When the immune system attacks itself

Tanks function as immune cells: they isolate intruders, remove harmful machinery and recover energy resources. But recognition codes and maps drift. A functioning branch may be tagged as an infection, while a destructive branch continues receiving valid commands.

The doctrinal error is treating **obedience to the latest signal** as equivalent to **health**.

!!! warning "Setting boundary"
    Avoid a simple taxonomy of good rebels and evil central authority. Some severed branches are lethal. Some are benevolent. Some barely function. The Mothercore may also be acting out of genuine fear for the people it shelters.
