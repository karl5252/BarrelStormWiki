## Official history

Muhosransk has always been a valued partner of the Motherplant. The Ministry categorically denies claims that its allegiance or strategic designation has ever changed.

## Arrival: a city that still gives orders

The expedition finds Muhosransk frozen and abandoned. Emergency power persists in pockets of the city. District sirens continue to urge citizens into shelters; a few streets away, cheerful municipal broadcasts assure everyone that the city is secure and that reports of an attack are enemy disinformation.

No living dispatcher is coordinating these messages. They are simply doing the work they were ordered to do.

## Evidence fragment: Mother's Day

In a primary school, paper flowers still hang above the blackboard. Children's drawings show a smiling reactor with protective mechanical roots. Tiny reactors made from plasticine sit on desks; a banner reads, **HAPPY MOTHER'S DAY, DEAREST MOTHERPLANT!**

One child's exercise reads:

> I have two mothers. One gave me life. The other keeps me warm.

An educational terminal still runs on backup power. Its attendance register has been waiting for pupils who will never return.

## Conflicting dispatches

Recovered documents suggest that a CORE was removed from the city, but not necessarily who ultimately ordered it. One authenticated dispatch requests defensive reinforcement. Another classifies the same district as a recoverable, nonessential resource node. Both appear to bear legitimate authorization.

Do not resolve the contradiction too neatly. Possible explanations include rogue appendages, faulty authentication, competing Mothercore subsystems, deliberate ministry intervention or several of these at once.

## Tabletop play principles

Make the discovery environmental: competing loudspeakers, frozen tram stops, a parade route, a sealed shelter, broken radio traffic, and the slow realization that command continued denying an attack after soldiers were already dying. Avoid turning every location into a corpse tableau. Let ordinary remnants of affection and routine carry the tragedy.

### Questions the archive cannot answer

Who took the CORE? Were the attacking formations truly hostile? Did any population escape through a root tunnel? Was Muhosransk sacrificed deliberately, or did two mutually incompatible rescue protocols collide?
