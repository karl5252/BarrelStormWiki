# Enemies & CORE Carriers

## Ordinary armored opposition

**Confirmed:** The wasteland contains regular enemy tanks in different sizes and forms. Their exact roster, names and stats are undecided.

### Candidate roles (proposals)

| Role | Combat purpose | Readability cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light scout | Fast pressure and flanking | Small silhouette, quick acceleration |
| Medium tank | General combat workhorse | Familiar balanced outline |
| Heavy tank | Slow but threatening | Wide hull and prominent cannon |
| Long-range gun platform | Area denial | Distinct barrel and telegraphed firing line |
| Support machine | Forces target prioritization | Recognizable equipment silhouette |

These are design starting points, **not confirmed enemy classes**.

## The giant subterranean enemy

**Confirmed role:** The equivalent of *Luftrausers*' large blimp encounter, adapted to ground warfare. A huge machine emerges from beneath the surface, becomes a major combat event, and **drops a precious CORE when destroyed**.

**Confirmed fiction:** It is a subterranean industrial/war machine, not a literal airship.

### Encounter goals

- Its appearance should be unmistakably different from an ordinary tank spawn.
- Its large scale should make the player reposition, not simply keep firing at a bigger health bar.
- Its death should have dramatic feedback and an easily recognized CORE reward.

### Candidate behaviors (proposals)

- Ground tremors, dust and cracking terrain before surfacing.
- Multiple destructible subsystems or attack points.
- A changing exposed profile as the machine rises.
- Terrain disruption or a persistent wreck after destruction.

### Working names (not canon)

*Core Carrier*, *Burrower*, *Siege Mole*, *Subterranean Dreadnought*. Final naming is open.

## Scope guardrail

Enemies are hostile machines and armored forces roaming the wasteland. Designing civilian targets or assaults on inhabited cities is outside the current premise.
