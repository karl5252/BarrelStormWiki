# Ordinary Life Under Mother

<div class="mp-kicker">MINISTRY OF MATERNAL WELFARE · DOMESTIC CULTURE AND APPROVED FESTIVITIES</div>

> **MOTHER IS NOT ONLY OUR PROTECTOR. SHE IS OUR HOME.**

This page describes ordinary routines rather than military doctrine. Most citizens experience the Motherplant through warmth, food, work, family and festivals, not through classified reports.

## Mother's Day

Mother's Day is celebrated in schools, communal kitchens and district halls. Children decorate rooms with paper flowers shaped like turbines and draw Mother as a smiling reactor whose roots embrace their homes. Some build tiny plasticine reactors with gold-painted cooling towers. Gifts are delivered to local intake boxes, where municipal personnel catalogue them for Mother's appreciation.

A popular classroom exercise asks children to name three things Mother provides. Common answers are *warmth*, *light* and *safety*. There is no compulsory fourth answer.

## The school outing

Children are taken on escorted visits to nearby energy galleries, where guides explain how much Mother sacrifices to keep the People comfortable. They are rarely shown the largest machines. Teachers call the distant pulses from the walls **Mother's heartbeat**.

A child who asks whether Mother gets tired may be praised for compassion and referred to the school counselor for unnecessary concern.

## Domestic customs

- Families place a small red ribbon beside the household heating gauge during winter.
- Weddings sometimes include the blessing: "May Mother keep your furnace burning."
- Households keep service anniversaries more reliably than birthdays, because the official calendar occasionally improves.
- A parent may comfort a frightened child by saying that the rumbling pipes are Mother turning over in her sleep.
- A holiday meal might feature root-vegetable stew, preserved mushrooms and a small cake in the shape of a furnace. Recipes vary with ration availability.

## The warmth ledger

Households receive official warmth allotments. The ledger is a source of pride as much as anxiety: to receive heat is to be known to Mother. Citizens sometimes help neighbors repair vents, share blankets and bring hot meals to a district that has been 'temporarily optimized'.

People can be generous even when the system governing them is cruel. The setting should remember this.

## Creative direction

Avoid depicting citizens as universally terrified or foolish. Many love their neighbors, their work and the Motherplant sincerely. That sincerity is what makes contradictions, silent sectors and abandoned schoolrooms matter.
