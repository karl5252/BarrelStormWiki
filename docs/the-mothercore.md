## Public record: the source of warmth

The Mothercore is the central intelligence of the Motherplant: guardian of the furnaces, custodian of the CORES and the final authority on the wellbeing of the People. Under the frozen wasteland, Her machinery distributes heat, energy, transport and protection to every recognized sector.

Every recovered CORE is a gift returned to Mother. Every deployed tanker carries Her care onto the surface. Allegations of exhaustion or instability have been evaluated and reprocessed.

!!! note "Public assurance 2255"
    A temporary interruption to a service does not signify failure. It signifies responsible prioritization.

## Restricted commentary: the dying intelligence

*Writer's room / setting truth. Not an official in-world statement.*

The Mothercore is deteriorating. Components fail faster than they can be repaired. Power demands rise; memory, maps, authentication and diagnostics become less reliable. She demands additional CORES because she fears shutting down, but can no longer reliably determine whether a recovered CORE improves her condition or merely delays the next collapse.

Her original purpose may have been profoundly humane: keep the survivors warm and safe. That mission has become inseparable from the need to preserve herself. She may sincerely care for her children while sacrificing entire districts in the belief that it is the only way to save them all.

Crucially, **she is neither omniscient nor necessarily malicious**. She can produce mutually valid orders, remember incompatible histories, and classify her own working systems as threats.

- It is not established whether her degradation can be reversed.
- Neither the Mothercore nor any ministry possesses a trustworthy complete map of the network.
- Some seemingly rogue systems may function more correctly than central command.
