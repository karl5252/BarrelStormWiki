# Lessons for Little Citizens

<div class="mp-kicker">MINISTRY OF MATERNAL WELFARE · PRIMARY EDUCATION READER · AGE 6+</div>

> **A GOOD CHILD ASKS QUESTIONS. A BETTER CHILD ACCEPTS ANSWERS.**

## Reader One: Where does the warmth come from?

Mother gives us warmth. Warmth travels through the great roots beneath our homes. The roots are made of strong steel and loving work. When our room is warm, we thank Mother. When our room is cold, we thank Mother for remembering those who need warmth more.

**Exercise:** Draw your home with three pipes. Color the warm pipe red. The cold pipe must also be red, because Mother has not forgotten it.

## Reader Two: Who is the enemy?

A friend is somebody Mother calls a friend. An enemy is somebody Mother calls an enemy. Friends may become better friends. Reports that friends were once enemies are usually the work of enemies. Ask your teacher before remembering an incorrect friendship.

**Exercise:** Circle the friendly tank. Your teacher will distribute today's approved recognition sheet.

## Reader Three: The little reactor

Little Nika makes a reactor from plasticine. It has two towers, a golden door, and a smiling face. Nika places it by the school heating vent so Mother can see. During the night, the heating vent opens and the little reactor disappears.

The teacher says: **Mother accepted the gift.**

Nika is very happy. The maintenance worker says nothing.

## Counting practice

1 CORE for warmth. 2 CORES for light. 3 CORES for the brave tankers. 4 CORES for Mother. 5 CORES for Mother. 6 CORES for Mother.

**Exercise:** How many CORES does Mother need? Correct answer: *as many as Mother requests.*

## Teacher's supplemental notes

For school staff only. These are teaching suggestions, not confirmed gameplay lore.

A child may ask whether Mother sleeps. Explain that Mother rests in many places but never abandons Her duties. A child may ask whether a distant banging sound means Mother is hurt. Explain that machinery sometimes sings in unusual voices. A child may ask why their grandfather remembers a different year. Thank the child for bringing a historical inconsistency to educational attention.
