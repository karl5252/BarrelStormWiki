# Tanker's Field Manual

<div class="mp-kicker">MINISTRY OF DEFENSE · ARMORED RESOURCE RECOVERY SERVICE · FIELD ISSUE</div>

> **EVERY TRACK MARK IS A SIGNATURE OF PROGRESS.**

## Your mission

The surface is an industrial recovery environment. Your unit has been entrusted with three sacred tasks: deploy, remove obstacles, and return valuable CORES to the Motherplant.

You do not engage in conquest. You conduct **forward-facing logistical clarification**.

## Deployment ceremony

Before deployment, tankers shall confirm their engine condition, ammunition allotment and gratitude. The underground carrier will transport the vehicle through Mother's protective roots. Personnel are not authorized to inspect the carrier's destination, origin or mechanical anatomy.

Some platforms are lifted by piston cradles. Others move along rail arteries. Others may be transported by articulated assemblies. All are correctly classified as **standard deployment infrastructure**.

## Identification in an uncertain theater

A hostile vehicle may resemble a loyal vehicle. Paint, insignia, radio call signs and registration papers are useful but secondary indicators.

**Only the current Ministry designation determines allegiance.**

A vehicle identified as allied yesterday is not necessarily the same vehicle identified as hostile today, even if the serial numbers match. This distinction is operationally essential and philosophically unnecessary.

## Recovering CORES

A CORE is not a prize and not private property. It is Motherplant property temporarily circulating beyond its assigned chamber. Large subterranean war machines may surface carrying these assemblies. Authorized personnel should recover CORES whenever tactical conditions permit.

Under no circumstances should tanker crews discuss why so many CORE-bearing machines display familiar industrial construction marks.

## Commendation

Soldiers whose actions exceed expectations may be named **Heroes of the Motherplant**. Recognition may include a parade, an official portrait, a brass fanfare and priority access to replacement uniform buttons.

## Field oath

> My engine is Her furnace. My tracks are Her roots. My orders are Her care. I shall return with the CORE, or be listed as having completed the assignment.

*Issued to all tank crews. Return this manual upon demobilization, retirement or other permanent service resolution.*
