# Art Direction & Interface

## Visual split

**Propaganda and menu framing:** preserve the neo-Soviet industrial look, severe typography, posters, Motherplant slogans and the garage/inventory presentation.

**Gameplay:** take cues from *Luftrausers* in silhouette clarity, limited-palette punch, kinetic firing effects and rapid recognition of enemies. This is inspiration for presentation and pacing, **not a directive to copy its artwork**.

## Battlefield principles

1. **Readability first.** Tanks, projectiles, pickups and dangerous terrain need distinct contrast at gameplay zoom.
2. **Controlled palette.** The provided prototype screenshots favor cream, dusty greens, charcoal and reddish-orange explosions. Refine that palette rather than adding visual noise indiscriminately.
3. **Explosive scale.** Shots and impact effects should feel forceful; giant emerging machines deserve a markedly greater visual footprint.
4. **Less HUD competition.** The battlefield and immediate hazards must remain legible. UI can carry lore outside combat.
5. **Surface as machinery's skin.** Tracks, cracks, dust and emerging structures communicate a world built over an active subterranean system.

## Screenshot review notes (October 2026 prototype)

- Existing tank sprite, track marks and explosion rings already support an appealing high-contrast arcade language.
- Checkered/patterned terrain seen in development shots may be placeholder or deliberate; the final texture language needs a decision.
- Landscape mobile layout uses two virtual touch controls; ensure neither obscures crucial projectiles, enemies or CORE drops.
- The garage mockup suggests part-based tank upgrades, but detailed equipment systems remain to be specified.
- UI timers in screenshots appear to be prototype data and are not established progression rules.

## Next art/UX decisions

- Target viewport and dynamic camera zoom for mobile versus desktop.
- Terrain pattern density and background-to-foreground contrast.
- Surface emergence warning sequence for giant CORE carriers.
- CORE color, silhouette and pickup feedback.
- Visual difference between ordinary patrols, elite tanks and major encounters.
